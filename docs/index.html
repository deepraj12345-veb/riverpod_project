Veg King - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

Veg King ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Veg King mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how your personal information is collected, used, and safeguarded.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, mobile phone number, and email address when you register or order.
  • Delivery Address: Street address, city, state, and pin code to accurately deliver your groceries and vegetables.
  • Location Data: Approximate or precise location with your consent, used strictly to check delivery coverage and auto-fill your address.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system, and crash diagnostics to ensure reliable app performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, fulfill, and deliver your orders to your doorstep.
  • To send order confirmations, delivery updates, and authentication OTPs.
  • To provide responsive customer support.
  • To ensure security and prevent unauthorized transactions.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is only shared with essential service providers:

  • Delivery Partners: Address and contact number shared strictly for delivering your order.
  • Payment Gateways: Payments are processed securely via RBI-compliant gateways (like Razorpay). We never store your debit/credit card details or banking passwords.

4. Data Security & Retention

We follow industry standards to safeguard your data. We retain personal details only as long as necessary for order fulfillment and legal requirements. Users can request account and data deletion at any time by emailing us.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
