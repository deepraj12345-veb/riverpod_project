# Privacy Policy for Veg King

**Last Updated:** September 19, 2026

Veg King ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Veg King mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how your personal information is collected, used, and safeguarded.

By downloading, installing, or using the Veg King App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

To provide our grocery and vegetable delivery services, we collect the following types of information:

- **Personal Information**: When you register or place an order, we may collect your name, mobile phone number, and email address.
- **Delivery Address**: We collect your street address, city, state, and pin code to deliver your orders accurately.
- **Location Information**: With your permission, we access your approximate or precise location solely to help auto-detect your delivery address and check service availability in your area.
- **Device & Usage Information**: We collect basic device information such as device model, OS version, and network connectivity to optimize app performance and troubleshoot technical issues.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
- To process, fulfill, and deliver your grocery and vegetable orders.
- To send order updates, delivery status notifications, and OTP verification codes.
- To provide customer care and respond to your inquiries.
- To detect and prevent fraudulent activities and improve app security.

---

### 3. Sharing of Information & Third-Party Services

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share data only with trusted service partners strictly necessary for providing our services:

- **Delivery Personnel**: Your name, delivery address, and phone number are shared with delivery partners strictly to deliver your order.
- **Payment Processing**: Online payments are processed securely through licensed payment gateways (such as Razorpay). We do not store your credit card, debit card, or banking credentials.
- **Legal Compliance**: We may disclose information if required by applicable law or legal process.

---

### 4. Data Security

The security of your personal data is important to us. We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations.
- Users have the right to request account deletion and removal of their personal data at any time by contacting our support team.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
